Methodology

End-to-end ML Pipeline

The full segmentation workflow runs automatically from raw data to strategic profiles.

STEP 01
📦
Data Ingestion
Load Mall Customers CSV with CustomerID, Gender, Age, Annual Income (k$), and Spending Score (1–100).
STEP 02
🔧
Feature Engineering
Select numeric features. Apply Min-Max scaling to normalize Income and Spending Score to [0, 1] range.
STEP 03
📐
Elbow Method
Compute WCSS for k=1..10. Identify the inflection point — confirmed at k=5 for this dataset.
STEP 04
🎯
K-Means Clustering
Run K-Means with k=5, 300 iterations, and k-means++ initialization. Assign cluster labels to each customer.
STEP 05
📊
Segment Profiling
Compute mean Age, Income, Spending Score per cluster. Derive persona names and targeted marketing actions.
